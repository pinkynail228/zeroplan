# 0.14 and 0.15 design: a lean zplan, one path and no dead weight

Status: revision 4 (2026-11-06), after two Goldfish passes on the design and one on the built tree:
- revision 1: `.local/goldfish/2026-11-07-0-14/`;
- revision 2: `.local/goldfish/2026-11-06-0-14-r2/`;
- the 0.14 tree (E): `.local/goldfish/2026-11-06-0-14-tree/`, resolved in section 9.

Only this header's 0.14 list and sections 8 and 9 are current. Sections 1 to 7 below are revision 2, kept as the
input for 0.15's design: where they say "0.14" they mean the single path that moved to 0.15.

Revision 2's second pass showed that the entry-point reroute, the line UI and the goal drafts are bound to the same
test fixtures as the old planner:
- the line UI's menu drives 64+ fixture tests;
- `/plan`, `/decline` and `/retry` drive 116 uses across 24 modules;
- each reroute raises consent and chat-availability questions (B1, B3).

Revision 3 therefore narrows 0.14 to work that changes no fixture and no consent rule. It moves the single path to
0.15, after the fixture migration, as one coherent design. The second pass's findings are 0.15's input (section 8).

**0.14 (this revision, built directly; reviewed as built):**
- **K. The decline defect.** Declining or revising the architect's plan with a note no longer reaches the old planner.
  - A proposed Goldfish revision is first stored as `declined` (`Planning.decline`, an optional revision key, valid
    on a proposed or approved revision). The architect's approve is refused for it at every autonomy level; the plan
    card asks the owner (`architect_may_approve`); the plan state and the status name the decline, so no yes in
    words binds it; only the owner's own card still approves it. It holds after a restart (B1).
  - The note is sent only after the decline is stored. An approve already queued before the decline runs first;
    the decline is then refused, nothing is sent, and the owner reads why, with his note back in the input.
  - Any other Goldfish revision (approved, a failed check) gets the note at once; no yes in words binds it anyway.
    The note is the owner's message "Revise plan r<N>: <note>".
  - When the chat cannot take the turn now, the whole message is added to the main screen's chat input (also under
    the plan screen), after what the owner typed; without a main screen the status line shows it (B3).
  - Without a chat (only with no project) and for an old-planner revision, today's path stays until 0.15.
  - Rollback: as with 0.12's `base_checks`, a store holding a declined revision cannot be opened by 0.13 or older; the
    installer's store copy is the rollback (`UPGRADE.md`, 0.14).
  - K is a stopgap: 0.15 may replace the free-text message with a structured request (M4). Alternatives weighed:
    a structured decline record read by the architect's method (a new tool and method text: deferred to 0.15's
    path design), and `revise_draft` (it keeps the old planner); the stored `declined` mark is the smallest change
    that makes consent hold in code.
- **D. Dead code.**
  - `foreman.py` goes with its tests (`test_foreman`, and `test_dirty_head`'s `ForemanTests`, K3/K4). They test
    foreman's own checks, which the critic's F1 replaces since 0.13.
  - `plan_tree.py` and `connections.py` go: no product importer.
  - `disk.py` goes with its test: no product importer, and its guard never arrived.
  - Unreferenced functions go: `checks._shut_fd`, `Delivery.stop_text`, `architect.as_dict`, and
    `chat._stamp_owner_line` with its `stamp` message. Functions that tests use as observers (`owner_actions`,
    `completed_review`, `chat_entry_lines`, `stop_pending`, `turn_thread`, and `_accepts` for `devtools/ui-lab/`)
    stay until 0.15's fixture migration, which decides each.
- **X. Linux leftovers.**
  - `devtools/linux-test.sh` goes, with `LinuxTestHostTest` and `LINUX_TEST` in `devtools/test_export_check.py`.
  - Its mentions in `devtools/fasttest.py`, `devtools/mac-test.sh`, `docs/UPGRADE.md` and `docs/INSTALLATION.md`
    become macOS-only text.
  - The `*_qual.py` scripts stay. `driver_qual.py` runs on macOS; `plan_qual.py` and `task_gate_qual.py` go with
    their features in 0.15.
  - Their export exemptions stay.
  - `toolchain.py` keeps its archive hashes, and setup refuses every platform but macOS arm64.
- **S. Docs and state.**
  - The cancelled plan-as-text and T2 designs move to the private archive. The test-system, egm-planning and a0-prompt
    designs stay: code and tests cite them as the source of live rules.
  - `ARCHITECTURE.md` is refreshed, and `STATE.md` is rewritten to current state only.
  - The contract already says "no separate planner" and "no line UI". It is left as it is until 0.15 makes them true.
- **E. Astra's evaluation of the tree:** `zoom_out`, `codebase_details`, and `design_critic` on `ARCHITECTURE.md`.
  Every Blocker and Major is resolved before the release. Its findings also feed 0.15's design.

Revision 2 follows, for 0.15's design.

## Revision 2 (the input for 0.15)

Terms:
- *The architect path*: the owner writes in the chat, the architect writes the plan with `zplan_plan` or
  `zplan_edit_plan`, the Goldfish plan check judges it, and the owner says yes.

  - The owner's Mac runs 0.10.0 (`STATE.md`, Production), and its stores hold old-planner revisions (v1 without
    `execution`, v2), task-gate records and goal drafts in transcripts.
  - Writers can go. Readers stay.

## 2. Goals

**0.14:**
1. **One planning path for the owner.** No owner, steward or architect action creates an old-planner revision. Each
   entry point in section 3 goes to the architect, or is removed.
2. **The dead code goes:** `foreman.py`, `plan_tree.py`, and the functions of appendix A, with their tests.
3. **Goal drafts are no longer written.** Stored transcripts that hold one still render it as text, as A0 3.7 asks.
4. **The line UI's menu goes.**
   - Every subcommand stays: `status`, `doctor`, `setup`, `login`, `logout`, `update`, `profile`, `roles`, `exec`.
   - Without Textual, zplan refuses with "reinstall zplan".
   - `cli.App` keeps the session core.
5. **The Linux leftovers go:** `devtools/linux-test.sh`, and the Linux qualification in `UPGRADE.md` and
   `INSTALLATION.md` (owner, 2026-11-05: macOS only).
6. **The docs and the state are current.**
   - Superseded designs move to the private archive.
   - `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `STATE.md` are rewritten.
   - `PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md` is amended where it names the old planner or a zplan graph editor (section 5, item 6;
     section 8, the graph editor line).
7. **Astra evaluates the tree**: `zoom_out`, `codebase_details`, and `design_critic` on `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**0.15:**
1. **Test fixtures stop using the old planner.** One shared helper builds an approved Goldfish revision from the
   fixture's Goldfish output. Each test module moves onto it with no change to its assertions.
2. **The old planner's writer code goes.** The readers stay, tested with a stored 0.10-era fixture: v1 and v2
   revisions, task-gate records, goal drafts.
3. **The task gate's writer goes.** `task_gate.py` keeps its readers, plus the protocol core the merge critic and the
   engine use (`STATUSES`, `TERMINAL`, `persistable`, `escaped`, `too_deep`, `validate_binding`, `terminal`,
   `submitted_title`, `SIZE`). Everything else goes.
4. **The planner-era settings are decided:** the `planner` role, `limits.plan_rounds`, `limits.task_rounds` and
   `gates.task`. Each is kept inert, or removed with a digest migration that a test proves does not make a pending
   approval stale.
5. **`devtools/zeroshot/` goes, with what it holds moved:**
   - the 26 recorded receipts go to the private archive;
   - the card contract goes to `docs/`;
   - the exemptions, the manifest and the tests are updated.

**Non-goals (both releases):**
- New behaviour.
- Splitting modules for size (AGENTS.md: by responsibility, not file size). After 0.15, a split is proposed only with
  named modules and the symbols each one gets, in its own design.
- Batch conversion of sentence assertions. A pin goes when it blocks a change (`DEVELOPMENT.md` section 4).
- Changing the plan check's documents: the Goldfish graph, runtime and input stay byte-identical (section 5).
- Deleting the owner's local data (`.dev-runs/`, the old branches) without his yes.
- Rewording owner-visible "planner" strings beyond what the removed paths take with them (a separate change).

## 5. Risks

- **Removing a reader.** Every 0.14 slice keeps the validators. 0.15's removals are tested against a stored 0.10-era
  fixture. Rollback: 0.14 writes no new record shape, so 0.13 opens a 0.14 store.
- **The plan check changes by accident.** A test pins the Goldfish graph, runtime and input documents byte-identical
  to 0.13 for a fixed context. The shared text (`planner_input_v2`, `verifier_instructions`, `V2_VERIFIER_TAIL`,
  `PLAN_CRITIC_BAR`, and `MERGE_CRITIC_TAIL`'s use of it) stays.
- **The owner's habits.** `/plan` and declining still work: they now go through the architect.
- **The size of the slices.** Each 0.14 slice above fits the slice limits. 0.15's fixture migration is split by test
  module.
- **Time.** No estimate in hours. Each slice is measured by its rounds, as the slice ledger records.
