# zplan direction after EGM

Status: decided by the owner on 2026-11-03 (revision 3). Revisions 1-2 proposed replacing Zeroshot with the
development slice runner; that idea is withdrawn. Their Goldfish reports are in `.local/goldfish/` (untracked).

## Decision

1. **Zeroshot stays zplan's execution engine.** Milestones keep running as Zeroshot graphs (worker, independent
   review, repair). Execution, verification (M0) and merge policy are unchanged by this decision.
2. **Planning follows the Elephant-Goldfish Model (EGM)** (<https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model>,
   Apache-2.0). The architect is the Elephant: it writes the plan itself. The separate LLM planner and its
   planner/critic rounds inside a Zeroshot graph go. Fresh model calls with no conversation (Goldfish) review the
   plan once: comprehension (a paraphrase of the plan), design critique and implementation readiness. Their output
   is stored with the plan revision and shown to the owner.
3. **EGM is adopted as modules, implemented inside zplan.** The plugin is not integrated as is: it assumes an IDE
   session with interactive gates and writes its own files, while zplan's deterministic controller owns revisions,
   the operation journal and idempotency, and the product architect runs one `codex exec` per turn. zplan reuses
   the EGM reviewer personas and document standards (vendored unmodified under `devtools/egm/`, to be moved into the
   product when R1 needs them) and implements the review step itself.
4. **The slice runner (`devtools/slice_run.py`) is a development tool only.** It runs Sonnet workers and Opus
   reviewers on zplan's own development; it is not part of the product and does not replace Zeroshot.

## Why

- The owner's motivation for the pivot was the planning stack: planner, planner/critic rounds and model gates
  added one after another. EGM addresses exactly that: one owner of intent, stateless review, decisions in a
  written artifact.
- The contract already says there is no separate planner (`docs/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md` section 2).
- The case for replacing Zeroshot was not supported by evidence (revision 2, section 2), and it would discard the
  engine the product is built on.

## Consequences

- Roadmap: `docs/plans/2026-11-03-roadmap.md`.
- Known obstacle for R1: every proposed or approved plan revision must carry a Zeroshot planner-run receipt
  (`terminal_planner/authority.py:601`); an architect-written revision needs its own provenance.
